# Neon Sky Runner

A 3D endless synthwave sky runner that runs entirely in the browser. Steer Mochi the jet-pack cat through three neon lanes, dodge laser gates, spiky orbs, asteroids and rings, and collect stars and power-ups (bubble shield, star magnet, rainbow boost).

Play: https://mirulismat-bit.github.io/neon-sky-runner/

Controls: Arrow keys or A/D to switch lanes, P to pause, M to mute. On mobile, swipe or tap left/right.

Single self-contained `index.html`; Three.js r147 loads from jsDelivr.
